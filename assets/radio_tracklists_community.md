[GTA Games Soundtrack]

[Twitter] [Reddit] [Copy Link]

Here you can explore all the music featured in the **GTA 6 Soundtrack**, discover all information about the **GTA** **6 Radio Stations known so far**, and listen to the confirmed GTA 6 songs directly on this page.

The full GTA 6 soundtrack and complete radio station tracklists have not been officially revealed yet. For now, this page brings together **every song and radio station confirmed so far.**

GTA 6 also introduces **on-demand podcasts** which, alongside radio, are available to listen **both in vehicles and on foot for the first time in the series**, through new headphones and earbuds connected to Jason and Lucia's smartphones.

This guide is a complete, playable database of the game's **radio stations, songs, and playlists**, with each station available to listen to directly from this page.

## About the GTA 6 Soundtrack

Music has always been a major part of the Grand Theft Auto experience, and GTA 6 is set to continue that tradition with a diverse soundtrack spanning multiple genres, artists, and radio stations.

The songs revealed so far already give us a glimpse of the musical atmosphere of [Vice City and Leonida], while the known and leaked radio station names provide an early look at the variety of music players can expect to hear while exploring the game.

The game has a Radio Wheel similar to the one from [Grand Theft Auto V], with a slow-motion effect to allow players to change stations safely.

This page will be continuously updated as new songs and stations are confirmed. At launch, our goal is to provide the **complete GTA 6 soundtrack**, including every radio station, genres, every available song, DJ hosts, and playable station tracklists.

Whether you're looking for a specific **GTA 6 song**, want to discover the **GTA 6 radio stations**, or simply want to listen to the game's soundtrack, you'll find it all here.

Rockstar Games has also officially [announced Grand Theft Auto 6: The Album], a 34-track collection that captures the electric energy of GTA 6's world of Vice City and Leonida. You can listen to the first six announced tracks from the album in the **Various Music** playlist.

## GTA 6 Soundtrack: Confirmed Songs from Trailers

Below are the officially confirmed tracks from the [two official trailers]. These are likely to be heard on one of the in-game radio stations:

### Trailer 1

- **Love Is a Long Road** – _Tom Petty (1989)_

Featured in Trailer 1, this rock ballad saw a ~37,000% spike in Spotify streams after the trailer’s release

### Trailer 2

- **Hot Together** – _The Pointer Sisters (1986)_

The main song in Trailer 2 starting at 1:04. It generated a 182,000% increase in Spotify streams

- **Thunder Island** – _Jay Ferguson (1977)_

A soft rock staple heard at the beginning of the trailer at 0:06

- **Child Support** – _Zenglen (2004)_

A Haitian konpa track heard at 0:27 while Jason drives.

- **Everybody Have Fun Tonight** – _Wang Chung (1989)_

Heard at 0:34 on a car radio; ties back to the original Vice City’s ‘80s pop vibe

- **Talkin’ to Myself Again** – _Tammy Wynette (1987)_

A country snippet heard in the trailer at 0:41

- **I Love Rock ’n’ Roll** – _The Arrows (1975)_

A classic rock track heard at 2:09 on the Ammu-Nation Commercial

### Launch Trailer (Trailer 3)

- **Flower in a Hurricane** – _Justice feat. Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard (2026)_

Brand new track by electronic duo Justice, also available on [Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album].
